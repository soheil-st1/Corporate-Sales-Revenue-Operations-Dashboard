# Corporate Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
I built this interactive Excel dashboard to analyze a dataset of corporate sales records. The goal was to break down $2.45M in total revenue and make it easy to see which products, regions, and sales channels are performing the best. 

## What I Did
* **KPI Cards:** Created quick-glance summary cards at the top for the most important metrics (Total Revenue, Total Orders, Quantity Sold, and Average Order Value).
* **Interactive Timelines:** Added timeline slicers so users can easily click through specific years, quarters, months, or even days to see how trends change.
* **Connected Slicers:** Linked standard slicers (Region, Sales Channel, Customer Type) to all the pivot charts so that clicking one button updates the entire dashboard at once.
* **Data Visualization:** Built line charts to show monthly revenue trends and bar/pie charts to compare product and regional performance.

## Key Insights
* **Top Product:** Computers are by far the biggest revenue driver, bringing in over $1M alone and beating all other categories.
* **Sales Channels:** Revenue is very well balanced across the business. Online sales lead slightly (32%), followed closely by Corporate Sales (29%) and Retail Stores (26%).
* **Regional Balance:** Sales are fairly even across the map, with the North and Central regions generating the most revenue.

## Skills Used
Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Dashboard Design, Slicers, Data Visualization.
